\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented MP-DNF, a deployed agentic pipeline that builds evidence-grounded, HS-code-typed \tool{}s of material transformations bottom-up. An annotation-free evaluation finds discovered processes almost always plausible, with full correctness limited mainly by HS-code assignment rather than by the discovery of processes; cited references support most hyperedges, mentioning at least one input and one output; canonical-route recall is high for boron but low for the other minerals; and each \tool{} covers many HS-6 codes absent from the USGS commodity listing, some of them shared utilities or code errors rather than mineral-specific intermediates. On the same \tool{}s, criticality rankings agree only moderately across \RboRuns{} boron generations, and coupling to UN Comtrade data isolates refined borax as the one concentrated boron intermediate whose loss cascades. The failure modes of Section~\ref{sec:lessons} motivate concrete next steps: HS-code assignment verified against the official nomenclature rather than a search tool's top hit, structured capture of inputs and outputs that currently survive only as narrative text, and seeded discovery to close coverage gaps in industrially central routes.
