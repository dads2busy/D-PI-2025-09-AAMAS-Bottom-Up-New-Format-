\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented MP-DNF, a deployed agentic pipeline that builds evidence-grounded, HS-code-typed \tool{}s of material transformations bottom-up. An annotation-free evaluation finds discovered processes almost always plausible. Two LLM judges disagree mainly on HS codes; an LLM-free nomenclature check shows the stricter one to be the more credible, and under it full correctness is limited mainly by HS-code assignment rather than by process discovery. Cited references support most hyperedges, mentioning at least one input and one output. Canonical-route recall is high for boron but low for the other minerals, and each \tool{} covers many HS-6 codes absent from the USGS commodity listing, some of them shared utilities or code errors rather than mineral-specific intermediates. Across \RboRuns{} boron generations, one of them the evaluated \tool{}, criticality rankings agree only moderately, and UN Comtrade data single out refined borax as the concentrated intermediate whose loss cascades furthest. The failure modes of Section~\ref{sec:lessons} point to next steps: verifying HS codes against the official nomenclature, capturing plain-name inputs and outputs as typed materials, and seeding discovery of industrially central routes.
